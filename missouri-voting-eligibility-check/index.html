Missouri Voting Eligibility Check: Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

The Missouri Voting Eligibility Check ("the extension") is a free browser extension from Free Our Vote, built in partnership with the Missouri Voter Protection Coalition. It helps community groups and nonprofit staff check whether a person's criminal record affects their right to vote in Missouri. This policy explains what the extension does with information.

The short version

  • Everything runs in your browser. There's no account, and Free Our Vote doesn't run any servers for the extension.
  • The extension sends nothing to Free Our Vote. It has no analytics, tracking, or ads.
  • The name you enter goes only to Case.net, the Missouri courts' public case records site, as part of the search.
  • Nothing about the people you search for is saved after you close the tab.

Information you enter

To run a check, you enter a person's first and last name and, if you know it, their date of birth (or birth year or age).

  • Name: sent to Case.net (courts.mo.gov) as a name search, from your own browser, in your own Case.net session. It's the same as typing the name into Case.net yourself.
  • Date of birth: never leaves your computer. It's used only in your browser, to match records.
  • What's on screen: the search, the records found, and the result exist only in the open browser tab. They're gone when you start a new search or close the tab.

Public records the extension reads

  • Missouri Department of Corrections: the extension downloads the Department of Corrections' public Sunshine Law data file directly from the state (docservices.mo.gov), at most once a day, and keeps a searchable copy in your browser's local storage so searches are fast and work offline. The file is public record. It's never sent anywhere else.
  • Case.net: the extension reads public court records from Case.net (courts.mo.gov) in a Case.net tab in your browser, using your own session there. Your use of Case.net is subject to the Missouri courts' own terms and policies.

What's stored on your device

  • The copy of the public Department of Corrections data described above, and the date it was downloaded.
  • A short timer, used when Case.net temporarily limits searches from your computer, so the extension waits before trying again.

Nothing else is stored. To remove all of it, uninstall the extension.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, receive, store, sell, or share any information you enter or any records you view.
  • We don't use analytics, cookies, advertising, or third-party code in the extension.
  • We don't use any information for purposes other than running the check you asked for.

Other sites

The result page links to case pages on Case.net and to the Free Our Vote and Missouri Voter Protection Coalition websites. When you open those links, the sites' own privacy policies apply.

Changes

If the extension changes how it handles information, we'll update this policy and the date above before the change takes effect.
